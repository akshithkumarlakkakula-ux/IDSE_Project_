How a realm works

Explore · Classify · Restore

1 · WalkMove with ← → (or A / D). Creatures, tablets and altars glow when something is waiting for you.
2 · Inspect & classifyStand next to it and press E. Read the features and decide the element. Correct answers open the door; wrong ones cost a heart.
3 · SurviveNull Wisps drift through every realm. Press Space to cast your power: shoot, shield, freeze or wall them off.
4 · Claim the rewardWhen the door glows, walk to it and press E. Every realm gives XP, a relic and sometimes a brand-new power.
